Hello,

Some people work “normal” hours…
Yet still feel emotionally exhausted.

Why?
Because burnout is not always caused by workload.

Sometimes it comes from:

Constant uncertainty
Meaningless meetings
Feeling like your effort changes nothing

📊 What do studies say?

In a Gallup study, one of the biggest causes of burnout was:

“Lack of control”

Not just long working hours.

⚖️ Why do people tolerate stress differently?

💪 One person works 12 hours and still feels okay
😞 Another burns out after only 6 hours

Mental energy is not drained by effort alone.

It is drained by:

Constant stress
Uncertainty
Being mentally “on alert” all the time

💡 Insight

People can tolerate fatigue better when they understand:
Why they are struggling… and where they are heading

But one of the hardest forms of exhaustion is:
Giving so much effort without clarity

✅ What actually helps?

1️⃣ Clear priorities

Not everything is urgent.

🎯 Clear direction
reduces emotional exhaustion

2️⃣ Reduce “invisible work”

Like endless follow-ups and random interruptions.

🧠 Mental interruptions
drain attention more than effort itself

3️⃣ Give people a sense of impact

Even small decisions can create a powerful emotional difference.

🤍 Final thought

Some workplaces are not exhausting because they are difficult.

They are exhausting because they:
consume your attention all the time
