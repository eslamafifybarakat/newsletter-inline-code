Hello,

There are people with "normal" jobs…
Yet they feel completely drained.

Why?
Because burnout isn’t always about having too many tasks.

Sometimes it’s about:

Constant vagueness
Meaningless meetings
Or feeling that your effort changes nothing

📊 What Do Studies Say?

According to a Gallup study, one of the main causes of burnout was:

"Feeling a lack of control"

Not just the number of hours.

⚖️ Why Do People Cope Differently?

💪 One person works 12 hours and feels relatively fine
😞 Another falls apart after only 6 hours

Mental energy isn’t consumed by effort alone.

It's consumed by:

Constant stress
Vagueness
And being mentally on alert all the time

💡 Insight

A person can tolerate fatigue better if they understand:
Why they are exerting… and where they are headed

But the most draining kind of exhaustion is:
Giving a lot… without any clarity

✅ What Really Helps?

1️⃣ Clear Priorities

Not everything is urgent.

🎯 Clarity of direction
reduces mental energy drain

2️⃣ Reduce "Invisible Work"

Like constant follow-ups and random replies.

🧠 Frequent mental interruptions
drain focus more than the effort itself

3️⃣ Give people a sense of impact

Even a small decision makes a big psychological difference.

🤍 A Final Thought

Some work environments don't tire you because they are hard.

They tire you because they:
consume your attention all the time
