💡 Development Corner

How Can You Improve Client Commitment?

  • Clarify the journey from the beginning
  • Connect sessions to a clear goal
  • Reduce ambiguity as much as possible
  • Recommend packages for better continuity
🗣️ Suggested Phrase:

"Let’s create a clear plan for the sessions so we can work on the issue gradually and achieve better results together."

🔔 Managing Free Sessions Effectively

To maximize the value of free sessions:

  • Clarify that the session duration is 5 minutes
  • Explain that it is for guidance, not a full therapy session
  • Provide initial practical steps for the client
  • Encourage follow-up through a scheduled session

📌 Recommended free session scheduling:

  • 2 years of experience or less: 2 hours weekly
  • 3+ years of experience: 1 hour weekly
