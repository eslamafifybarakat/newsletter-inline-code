📅 Client Experience & Organization

Appointment Organization & Quality Experience

We emphasize the importance of avoiding appointment cancellations whenever possible. If the appointment time is no longer suitable, we encourage using the rescheduling option instead of cancellation.

1️⃣ Prevent overlapping or accumulated appointments
2️⃣ Improve client experience
3️⃣ Enhance organizational quality within the platform
